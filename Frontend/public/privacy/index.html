YOUR INFORMATION AT HIREEDGE

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

HireEdge helps you tailor resumes, understand job fit, and practice interviews. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the HireEdge website.

The essentials. We use account information to sign you in and save your work. The career information you submit is processed by AI providers to generate your requested results. For privacy questions or account deletion requests, email ashifrza33@gmail.com.

Information collectedGoogle sign-inService providersYour choices

1. Information you provide

  • Account details: your username, email address, sign-in method and verification status; your phone number if you use phone verification.
  • Career information: uploaded resume content, self-descriptions, job descriptions and any personal details included in them.
  • Your workspace: generated reports and resumes, interview questions, submitted practice answers, feedback, scores and progress.
  • Support messages: information you send when you contact us.

Uploaded resume PDFs are processed in server memory to extract text. The extracted text is saved with your report. Avoid uploading information you do not want processed, including unnecessary sensitive information about yourself or others.

2. Google sign-in information

When you choose Google sign-in, HireEdge receives a Google ID token containing basic profile information. We verify the token and use your Google account identifier, email address, email verification status and name to create or identify your account. Your name may be used to generate a username.

HireEdge stores your Google account identifier, email address and account verification details with your account. This sign-in flow does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or Google password. Google sign-in credentials are not included in our AI-generation requests. Personal details in a resume or answer you submit can be included in those requests.

3. How information is used

We use information to authenticate users, deliver verification codes, generate resume and interview assistance, save reports and practice sessions, respond to support requests, and diagnose errors or prevent abuse. HireEdge does not include an advertising or data-sale feature.

4. Service providers and sharing

Information is processed by services needed to deliver the features you use:

  • Google: account authentication and delivery of Google sign-in components. The website also loads fonts from Google.
  • OpenAI and Groq: resume text, job descriptions, self-descriptions, practice answers and relevant report context may be sent to generate or evaluate results. The service can use Groq as a fallback when an OpenAI request fails.
  • Email and SMS providers: the configured email delivery provider processes your email address and verification message; Twilio processes your phone number and verification requests when phone sign-in is used.
  • Hosting and database services: Render hosts the deployed application; the configured database stores account records, reports and practice sessions. Infrastructure providers can process connection details such as IP addresses, browser information, request times and operational logs.

Provider processing, storage locations and retention depend on the provider and account configuration, and may involve processing outside your country. Their privacy terms also apply to their services. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

5. Cookies and optional voice input

HireEdge uses a sign-in cookie to maintain your session. The current session cookie expires after one day. Google components may use their own cookies or similar browser technologies.

If you activate voice input for interview practice, your browser requests microphone access and converts speech into text. Depending on the browser, its speech-recognition provider may process audio remotely. HireEdge submits the resulting answer text for evaluation when you choose to submit it; its practice feature does not upload an audio recording to the HireEdge backend. You can type instead and revoke microphone permission in your browser settings.

6. Storage and retention

Account records, saved resume text, reports and practice sessions remain in the database unless deleted; they do not currently have a scheduled automatic expiry. Email verification codes expire after ten minutes and the verification record is removed after successful use. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules.

Deleting a report removes that report from your workspace, but does not automatically delete your account or all related practice-session records. Contact us if you want those records removed too.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose what career information to submit, use typed answers instead of voice input, and delete individual reports through your workspace. You can review or remove HireEdge's Google connection in your Google Account settings. Removing the Google connection does not by itself delete information already stored in HireEdge.

To request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated data, email ashifrza33@gmail.com from your account email where possible. We may need to verify ownership before acting. Do not send your password, sign-in code or Google token. The handling of a request may depend on applicable legal requirements and backup or provider retention.

8. Security and policy updates

HireEdge uses controls such as verified sign-in tokens, HTTP-only session cookies and authentication checks for workspace access. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version will be available on this page with its updated date.

9. Contact HireEdge

For questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact the HireEdge operator at ashifrza33@gmail.com.
